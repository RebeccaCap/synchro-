Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

SynCHRO Forum ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting the personal data of everyone who interacts with our website and events. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable European data protection law.

1. Who we are

SynCHRO is a European CHRO forum, co-chaired by Capgemini and Equinor. Our website is hosted at synchroforum.eu. For data protection enquiries, please contact us at info@synchroforum.eu.

2. What data we collect

When you submit an RSVP through our website, we collect the following personal data:

  • First and last name
  • Work email address
  • Organisation name
  • Attendance confirmation (attending or not attending)
  • Dietary or access requirements (if provided voluntarily)

We do not collect any data beyond what you provide through the RSVP form. We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics tools on this website.

3. Why we collect it

We collect your data solely for the purpose of organising and administering the SynCHRO forum. Specifically, to:

  • Confirm your attendance at the event
  • Contact you with logistical information regarding the forum
  • Accommodate any dietary or access requirements you have shared

The legal basis for processing your data is your consent, given at the time of submitting the RSVP form (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), and our legitimate interest in organising and communicating about the event (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. How we store and protect your data

RSVP responses are stored in a secure log file on our web server, hosted by Hostinger. Access to this data is restricted to the event organisers via a password-protected administration page. We do not store payment information or any sensitive personal data as defined under Article 9 of the GDPR.

5. Who we share your data with

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties for marketing or commercial purposes. Data may be shared on a need-to-know basis between Capgemini and Equinor solely for the purposes of organising the SynCHRO forum.

6. How long we keep your data

We retain your RSVP data for no longer than 12 months following the event to which it relates. After this period, the data is securely deleted.

7. Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@synchroforum.eu.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects when it was last revised. Continued use of the website following any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact

For any questions regarding this privacy policy or how we handle your data, please email info@synchroforum.eu.
